# [view]: docs/04 3.4 table, used when the plan has one direction equal to the view default.
[view]
side | Strict side view (profile), character facing right. Orthographic, no perspective.
topdown | Top-down RPG view from about 45 degrees above, character facing down toward the viewer.
3/4 | Three-quarter view, character facing down-right.
front | Front view, character facing the viewer.
rear | Rear view, character facing away from the viewer.
# [prefix] + [direction]: used for multi-direction plans (and single non-default directions).
[prefix]
side | Strict side view, orthographic, no perspective;
topdown | Top-down RPG view from about 45 degrees above,
3/4 | Three-quarter view,
front | Front view,
rear | Rear view,
[direction]
down | character facing down toward the viewer. The front of the body, face and chest are visible.
up | character facing up, away from the viewer. Only the back of the head, back, and rear of the clothing are visible; the face is not visible.
right | character facing right, profile. Only the right side of the body is visible.
left | character facing left, profile. Only the left side of the body is visible.
